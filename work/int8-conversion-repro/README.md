# Fresh signed-integer conversion reproduction

`Fresh.v` imports the dependency closure of the original `Int8.toBitVec_not` declaration from line 1. `Adjacent.v` independently imports the corresponding Int16/Int32/Int64/ISize negation theorems and Int8 and/or/xor theorems. Neither driver loads a prefix or checkpoint, skips declarations, or replaces a Lean proof. Both use strict failure and a 30-second per-line timeout.

The original declarations are `rfl` proofs generated by `declare_bitwise_int_theorems` in Lean's `src/Init/Data/SInt/Bitwise.lean`, lines 23–26. Their original bodies and dependencies are exported directly; there is no synthetic theorem or altered Lean source.

## Kernel repair

The previous worker timed out at `Int8.toBitVec_not`, reduced export line
22,341 (full CSLib line 3,842,943), with a 30-second declaration timeout.
Conversion call 2060 exposed the `BitVec.not` constructor while leaving
`Int8.toBitVec` folded. Early record eta then compared fields and descended
into `Nat.bitwise` and well-founded recursion.

The initial repair made delta precede general record eta. It passed the Int8
checks but **regressed DHashMap**: the user's `eta-review` gate timed out on
`Std.DHashMap.Const.unitOfList_cons`. The full CSLib run never started.
The controlled reproduction and before/after evidence are in
[the DHashMap report](../dhashmap-eta-repro/README.md).

The corrected repair preserves early record eta and extends the existing
constructor-wrapper exception to projection wrappers, following at most 32
transparent head aliases. This includes the named UInt32 projection used by
Int32. Other record-producing computations keep eta before unfolding. Opaque
bodies remain opaque, and every result still goes through ordinary conversion.

No importer, original Lean proof, arithmetic reduction, universe constraint,
line timeout or transparency rule changed. The experimental kernel's existing
unit-field checks and ordinary stuck-record eta fallback remain intact.

The baseline source and worker from before the original Int8 repair remain

Each invocation below builds a fresh foundation and imports from line 1 under

ROCQ_TEST_BASELINE=1 bash work/int8-conversion-repro/run.sh Fresh baseline-check

The baseline command is expected to time out at `Int8.toBitVec_not`.
The kernel regression fixtures cover:

- `ProjectionBeforeEta.v`: direct projection wrappers, both directions.
- `OpaqueUnitRecord.v`: opaque and stuck records with unit fields, including
  rejection of unequal relevant fields.
- `../dhashmap-eta-repro/EtaBeforeComputation.v`: eta without evaluating an
  expensive producer; the broad-eta worker times out at its first kernel check.
- `../dhashmap-eta-repro/ProjectionAliases.v`: named projection wrappers and
  genuinely opaque aliases; the direct-projection-only candidate times out.

Copies of these four fixtures are in the kernel's `test-suite/success/`.
`ProjectionWrappers.v` supplies additional operation/projection controls.
